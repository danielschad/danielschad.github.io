Research Outcome Preferences

A short questionnaire on how researchers value different research outcomes

Thank you for taking part. This questionnaire takes about 10 minutes.

About this questionnaire

This is an informal pilot for a planned research project on how researchers value research outcomes, conducted by Daniel J. Schad (HMU Health and Medical University Potsdam). Participation is voluntary and anonymous: we do not ask for your name, your email address, or any other information that identifies you.

Nothing is sent until you click “Submit” at the end, so you can stop at any time by closing this page. Your answers are then sent to Daniel J. Schad by email via the form service Web3Forms and will be used only to improve the scenarios for the planned study.

Questions? Please contact Daniel J. Schad.

What to do

On the following pages you will see eight pairs of research outcomes from the cognitive sciences. For each pair, imagine that both outcomes are possible and that you must choose which one you would prefer to happen.

There is no right answer. We are interested in your preferences, not in what you think you ought to say.

Please answer each pair twice:

  • once for your own research: which outcome would you prefer to happen in your own work?
  • once for your field as a whole: which outcome would you prefer to happen in your research field generally?

Click your choice for each question. Your two answers may differ, and often will.

Background

All scenarios concern a study comparing two hypotheses: H0, that there is no effect, and H1, that an effect of a size derived from the prior literature exists.

Each scenario tells you what the team concluded and, unusually, what was actually true. Please take the stated facts exactly as given, including the citation counts, even where they differ from what you might expect in practice. “Person-years” refers to combined supervisor and PhD student time. Citation counts are for the first five years after publication.

If a pair seems impossible to answer, please choose anyway and note why in the comment box.
